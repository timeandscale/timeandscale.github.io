Time & Scale

How places change over time — and how big things really are.

Time & Scale is a YouTube Shorts channel with two series:

  • Street View time travel — the same house across the decades, from the 2000s to today.
  • True-scale size comparisons — how many countries fit on the Moon, Mars or Pluto, and how countries compare to each other.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@timeandscale

Time & Scale uploader

The channel is published with a small private tool, Time & Scale uploader, run only by the channel owner on their own computer. It uses Google sign-in to upload the channel's own videos and to read the channel's own YouTube Analytics. It has no other users.
