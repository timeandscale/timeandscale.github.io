Privacy Policy

Time & Scale uploader · effective October 25, 2026

Time & Scale uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Time & Scale YouTube channel to publish that channel's videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What Google data the app accesses

After the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these permissions for the owner's own account only:

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload) — to upload the channel's own Shorts.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly) — to confirm the channel and read the status of its own uploads.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly) — to read view, retention and like statistics of the channel's own videos.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload the channel's videos and to measure how they perform, so future videos can be improved.
  • OAuth tokens and statistics are stored locally on the channel owner's computer. Nothing is stored on any server operated for the app.
  • The data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.
  • No data about viewers or any other Google user is collected.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; locally stored tokens are then deleted.

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking.
